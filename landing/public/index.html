01 / THE APPROACH

Dig deeper.
Keep the trail intact.

Research should help you understand what the sources actually support—and where they stop.

  1. 01

    Start with the right question.

    A résumé, a professional claim, or a research brief gives the investigation its starting point. Identity anchors keep the work focused on the right subject.

  2. 02

    Follow the public record.

    The investigator searches, reads, and follows leads across public web pages, repositories, archives, professional profiles, and relevant records.

  3. 03

    Make the findings reviewable.

    Reports connect conclusions to captured sources. Established, partial, unresolved, conflicting, and contradicted findings remain distinct.

02 / THE OUTPUT

More than an answer.
A record of how you got there.

A plain-text report brings the findings, source links, and unanswered questions together. An evidence-support score describes support for the reviewed claims; it does not rate a person's trustworthiness.
