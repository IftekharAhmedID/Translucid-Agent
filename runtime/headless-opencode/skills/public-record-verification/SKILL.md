---
name: public-record-verification
description: Verify claims against authoritative public records with identity and date checks.
compatibility: opencode
---

Load this method only for patents, standards, publications, packages, filings, events, education, or other institutional records. Choose the route matching the record type, then confirm the named person or account with identity anchors and preserve the record's own dates and roles. Prefer the issuing institution or canonical registry over summaries. A matching name alone is insufficient, and current absence does not disprove a historical record. Use one record across every directly supported facet without duplicating provider calls.
