---
name: source-evaluation
description: Evaluate public sources, exact quotes, authority, and independence without overstating evidence.
compatibility: opencode
---

Load this method only when choosing or checking evidence. Treat gateway-provided authority and independence metadata as final. Search results are discovery only; cite an immutable evidence-eligible source. Use `source.excerpts` for exact stored wording and never reconstruct a quote from separate fields. A source may support several facets when each evidence edge maps only the facets its exact quote establishes. Prefer direct work, institutional records, and independent professional reporting over self-representation. Stop after direct authoritative evidence or two independent attestation groups unless credible conflict remains. Missing public evidence is `UNRESOLVED`, never suspicion.
