---
name: source-evaluation
description: Evaluate public sources, exact quotes, provenance, and uncertainty without overstating evidence.
compatibility: opencode
---

Load this method only when choosing or checking evidence. Search results are discovery only; cite an immutable captured source and use `source.excerpts` for exact stored wording. Never reconstruct a quote from separate fields. Evaluate each material predicate for identity fit, temporal fit, directness, publisher authority, source family, independence, contradiction, alternative explanations, and negative-evidence coverage. Treat résumé, LinkedIn, personal-site, and candidate-written institutional biographies as one candidate-origin family unless institutional authorship is evident. Prefer employer/registrar-owned records, direct work, institutional records, and genuinely independent reporting over self-representation. Record what each source establishes, what it cannot establish, and why search stopped. Missing public evidence is status `0`, never suspicion or contradiction.
