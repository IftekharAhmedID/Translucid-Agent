---
name: source-evaluation
description: Evaluate public sources, exact quotes, provenance, and uncertainty without overstating evidence.
compatibility: opencode
---

Load this method only when choosing or checking evidence. Search results are discovery only; cite an immutable captured source and use `source.excerpts` for exact stored wording. Never reconstruct a quote from separate fields. A source may support several findings when its exact text establishes each one. Prefer direct work, institutional records, and genuinely independent reporting over self-representation, and record the limits of public evidence explicitly. Missing public evidence is status `0`, never suspicion.
