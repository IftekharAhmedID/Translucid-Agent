Load this method only for employment claims. Keep employer, title, team, location, and start/end dates distinct even when grouped in one coherent claim. Separate current profile state from historical evidence, prefer contemporaneous employer or institutional records, and use archives only for a material missing interval or conflict. Overlapping dated assertions may conflict; sequential title changes usually show progression. Corroborating an employer does not prove title, location, responsibility, or exact tenure. Preserve unsupported internal responsibility as an explicit unresolved detail.

In the final gap pass, select no more than three highest-impact unresolved historical employment or responsibility claims. Spend at most four provider calls per selected claim and use at least two applicable route classes: employer/title/year; technology/person/employer; public mailing lists, forums, or discovered archives; event or conference material; résumé mirrors or institutional records. Fetch decisive leads directly and stop when a dispositive record is captured. If the gap remains, retain a precise unresolved claim rather than padding citations. This bounded pass is for historical recovery only; do not add query telemetry, databases, RAG, agents, or a second model.
