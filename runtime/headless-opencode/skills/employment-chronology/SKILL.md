name: employment-chronology
description: Verify employer, title, location, and tenure as separate time-aware employment facets.
compatibility: opencode
---

Load this method only for employment claims. Keep employer, title, team, location, and start/end dates distinct even when grouped in one coherent claim. Separate current profile state from historical evidence, prefer contemporaneous employer or institutional records, and use archives only for a material missing interval or conflict. Overlapping dated assertions may conflict; sequential title changes usually show progression. Corroborating an employer does not prove title, location, responsibility, or exact tenure. Preserve unsupported internal responsibility as an explicit unresolved facet.
