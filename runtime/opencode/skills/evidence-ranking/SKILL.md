---
name: evidence-ranking
description: Rank source strength and independence without overstating it.
---
Treat the gateway's source authority and independence group as final; never supply or promote your own tier. Prefer direct work, first-party institutional and contemporaneous records, then independent professional sources, self-representation, and context. Search discovery artifacts are never evidence. Syndications, provider views of one profile, archives of one domain, repository API/clone views, shared DOI records, and shared CVE records remain one source family. Exact quotes and locations must exist in immutable artifacts. Stop after direct authoritative evidence or two genuinely independent corroborating families unless material contradictory evidence remains. Absence is usually a limitation, not contradiction.

Before every external call, identify verified facets, missing facets, the exact predicate/value/time gap, missing source family, exhausted routes, and the route capable of filling the gap. After each useful source, capture exact evidence, record observations, compare the source against every existing unit and facet, and create a separate evidence row for every directly supported claim; never use `evidence.link` to attach a claim. Recalculate the gap after each capture and stop when the evidence threshold is met.

Use multi-lens search selectively: exact phrase, person/project/company, reverse project lookup, authoritative domain, technical artifact, package/institutional, or time-qualified search. Exa discovery is highlight-first: use fast/auto search with 3–5 targeted results and store highlights separately from citable source artifacts; fetch full pages only when highlights cannot establish the required wording, chronology, or context. Do not research every lens automatically.
