---
name: social-relevance
description: Decide when narrow public social research is justified.
---
Allow social research only for an explicit social claim, a public cross-link needed for identity, or a material activity question whose active frontier names that route. Use the least invasive configured platform route and stop after the relevant fact or cross-link is captured. Do not browse because a profile might exist, and do not duplicate a resolved professional identity route. Never infer protected traits or personality.
