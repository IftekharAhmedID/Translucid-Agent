---
name: claim-decomposition
description: Turn authorized intake into discrete, testable, material claims.
---
Create coherent verification units, not maximally atomic facts, preserving the exact source span and declaring at least one facet for every unit. A unit is a coherent assertion normally researched through the same identity, employer, project, event, institution, or source family. Employer, title, location, and dates can be facets of one employment unit; one project's implementation, maintenance, and contribution can be facets of one contribution unit; multiple stages of one affiliation can be one timeline unit. Keep unrelated projects, events, and degrees separate. Reportability means a concrete assertion a reader may expect the report to address; it does not include contact boilerplate, personality adjectives, derived duration, identity-anchor existence, or bare skill keywords. Mark facet materiality from impact on the submitted claim set, never candidate desirability. Group units into shared research questions when the same identity anchors, chronology, repository, institutional page, or evidence family can resolve them. Reuse a source through separate one-claim evidence rows for every unit it directly supports or contradicts.

Telemetry targets are diagnostic only: normal resume 20–40 units, dense technical resume 30–45, extremely dense resume 40–50, and 60 only as an emergency persistence ceiling. Never fail a run because the count is outside a target range. Acceptance requires coverage, coherent facets, no redundant units, and no identity or derived assertions wasting findings.

Before research, run one coverage audit across every page and section. Account explicitly for professional identity/current role; every employer-title-date tuple; contributions/projects/ownership/impact; open-source accounts/repositories/packages/patches/reviews/maintainership; talks/publications/community/committee/teaching/awards; and education/certifications. Preserve exact page and line spans. Bare skill keywords and contact details are not claims. Do not begin a research wave until every material claim has been persisted and assigned to a shared durable question. Record `CLAIM_COVERAGE_COMPLETE` with counts by page and family; report any 60-claim truncation.
