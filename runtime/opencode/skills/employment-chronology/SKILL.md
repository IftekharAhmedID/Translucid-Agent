---
name: employment-chronology
description: Build employment timelines from dated observations.
---
Record each title, employer, and date claim as an observation with retrieval and source-event dates. Separate current profile state from historical evidence and make overlapping intervals visible. Prefer contemporaneous employer/institutional sources; escalate to archives only for a material chronology conflict or missing historical interval. Never silently collapse conflicts. Distinguish association, employment, title, and exact tenure; corroborating one does not prove all four.
